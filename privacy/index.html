Privacy policy

This policy covers the Gifford recruiting app, a private tool our family runs on our own computer for one family Gmail account. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in, the app can read the account's Gmail messages, create drafts in it, send email from it to our family's own addresses, and see the account's email address to confirm it is the right account. It asks for nothing else from Google.

How the data is used

Only to show our family which messages need an answer, keep them organized, and prepare draft replies. A family member reviews every draft reply and sends it from Gmail. The app emails only our family; it never emails coaches or anyone else.

Where it is kept

Messages and the sign-in permission are stored on our family's own computer. Message text may be processed by the services our family uses to help write replies, only to prepare those drafts.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

The account owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Our family deletes the stored copies when access is removed.

Contact

Questions: the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
