Gifford recruiting app

The Gifford recruiting app is a private tool our family runs on our own computer. It helps us keep up with one family Gmail account: it reads incoming messages so we can see what needs an answer, keeps them organized, and prepares draft replies in Gmail.

A family member reviews every draft reply and sends it from Gmail. The app emails only our family; it never emails coaches or anyone else.

It is used only by our family and is not offered to anyone else. It connects to Gmail through Google's sign-in, and the account owner can remove that access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
