Quick answersQuestions coaches ask

Where are my field goal attempts?

Fair question. Our offense likes to keep the ball: over the last four seasons, Wells went for two after about nine of every ten touchdowns and went for it on about nine of every ten fourth downs. So on Friday nights most of my work is kickoffs, and my field goals get charted at ranking camps. At the Chris Sailer Kicking LA Fall Camp in October I made them from 55 and 60 and shared the field goal title.

So what do the kickoffs look like?

40 touchbacks in the 2026 season. The film above has them one play at a time.

And when one gets returned?

I want to be the one making the hit. Here's one against Jefferson.

How does a soccer player end up kicking?

My soccer team held a field goal contest. I won it, then tried out for football. I played club soccer with United PDX from the first year I was old enough and took penalty kicks for my teams, so I like being the one who scores when the team needs points.

What am I working on?

Getting every kickoff to four seconds of hang time.

In my wordsAbout me

I always wanted to play football, and kicking is my chance. Before football I played soccer, for United PDX and for Wells.

Long field goals and kickoffs out of the end zone are my favorite kicks, and when one does get returned, I want to be the one making the hit. Right now I'm working on getting every kickoff to four seconds of hang time.

In college I'm looking to compete against the best, be part of a program and keep getting better. I'd kick field goals and kickoffs at a school where I can take a hard science major on a pre-med track.
