About me

I always wanted to play football, and kicking is my chance. I played club soccer with United PDX from the first year I was old enough, and high school soccer at Wells. When my soccer team held a field goal contest, I won it and then tried out. I took penalty kicks for my soccer teams, and I like being the one who scores when the team needs points.

Long field goals and kickoffs out of the end zone are my favorite kicks, and when one does get returned, I want to be the one making the hit. Right now I'm working on getting every kickoff to four seconds of hang time.

In college I'm looking to compete against the best, be part of a program and keep getting better. I'd kick field goals and kickoffs at a school where I can take a hard science major on a pre-med track.

Off the field I'm vice president of my school's HOSA (Future Health Professionals) chapter, 80 members, my second year in the role, and an American Red Cross certified lifeguard; I worked as a lifeguard in summer 2026.
