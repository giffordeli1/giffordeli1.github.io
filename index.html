FAQ

Quick answers

Where are the field goal attempts?

Fair question. The offense likes to keep the ball: in 2026, Wells went for two 51 times and went for it on fourth down 27 times. So on Friday nights most of the work is kickoffs, and field goals get charted at ranking camps. At the CSK Southern California Fall Camp in October I made them from 55 and 60 and shared the field goal title.

So what about the kickoffs?

40 touchbacks in the 2026 season. In 2027 I'm going to set a state record for touchbacks in a single season.

And when one gets returned?

I want to be the one making the hit. Here's one against Jefferson.

How does a soccer player end up kicking?

It started with a field goal contest on the soccer team. I won it, then tried out for football. I played club soccer with United PDX from the first year I was old enough and took penalty kicks, so I like being the one who scores when the team needs points.

What am I working on?

More hang time on every kickoff.
